3. Information We Collect

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Google OAuth Identity (Optional): If you choose to sign in with Google for cloud backup, we access your basic profile (Name, Email, Profile Picture, and optional Birthday for birthday countdowns).
  • Locally Stored Content: To-dos, habits, notes, custom budget logs, and events are stored in your device's local sandboxed memory.

B. Third-Party Services & SDKs

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Used to deliver banner and interstitial ads in the free version. Personalized or non-personalized ads are served based on user consent. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Drive AppData API: Used exclusively to synchronize your pinned tools. We have zero access to your personal files.
  • Open-Meteo & Yahoo Finance APIs: Anonymous network requests for weather and public market indices.

4. User Rights & Account / Data Deletion

Google Play mandates that users can request account and data deletion at any time. In Utilio, you have complete control:

  • In-App One-Click Deletion: Go to Settings → DATA & PRIVACY → Delete Account & Cloud Data to immediately wipe your synced cloud data and sign out.
  • Clear Cache: Tap Clear Temporary Cache in Settings to wipe all locally stored API caches.
  • Revoke Google Access: You can revoke Utilio's Google OAuth permission at any time in Google Account Permissions.
  • Email Request: Contact us at tanimsarwar@gmail.com to request immediate manual deletion.
