Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 18, 2026

1. Overview & Privacy-First Architecture

Welcome to Utilio ("we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to delivering a seamless productivity, utilities, finance, and wellness multi-tool experience while prioritizing your personal privacy.

  • On-Device First: Most utilities (calculators, unit converters, password generator, drawing pad, timers, notes, and local trackers) function entirely on your device without sending private data to external servers.
  • Minimal Permissions: We only request hardware permissions that are strictly necessary to deliver features you interact with.
  • Zero Data Selling: We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to third parties.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Google Account Identity (Optional): If you choose to sign in with Google to synchronize your favorite tools across devices, we receive basic identity information (name, email address, profile photo, and optional birthday used solely for countdown calculations).
  • User Content: Notes, tasks, habit records, custom budgets, and events are stored locally on your device (and optionally in your private Google Drive AppData folder if cloud sync is enabled).

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Identifiers & Diagnostics: Anonymous device model, operating system version, and interface preferences to ensure optimal layout scaling.
  • Advertising Identifiers: In the ad-supported free version, Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) processes advertising identifiers in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies.

4. Third-Party Services

Utilio integrates with trusted third-party SDKs and public APIs:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Serves banner and interstitial ads. Ads can be permanently disabled via the Ad-Free Pro upgrade. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Drive API (AppData): Used to backup pinned preferences in a private sandbox. We have zero access to your personal files.
  • Open-Meteo API: Anonymous coordinate queries for weather and air quality.
  • Yahoo Finance API: Anonymous queries for public market indices and stock prices.

5. Data Retention & Account / Data Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Clear Cache: In Settings → DATA & PRIVACY → Clear Temporary Cache, you can instantly wipe all cached data.
  • Account & Cloud Data Deletion: If you signed in with Google, you can purge your cloud preferences and sign out at any time by selecting Settings → DATA & PRIVACY → Delete Account & Cloud Data.
  • Google Account Permissions: You can revoke Utilio's access at any time via Google Security Settings.
  • Direct Request: You may also email tanimsarwar@gmail.com to request complete data deletion.

6. Health & Financial Disclaimers

  • Wellness Disclaimer: The BMI Calculator, Breathing exercises, Sleep tracker, and Meditation tools are intended strictly for educational and wellness purposes and do not constitute professional medical advice.
  • Financial Disclaimer: Stock market prices, loan calculators, and tax estimators are provided for estimation purposes only and do not constitute financial, investment, or tax advice.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Utilio does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately for deletion.
