# Privacy Policy for Utilio

**Last updated:** August 18, 2026

Welcome to **Utilio** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a secure and seamless experience across all our productivity, finance, wellness, and utility tools.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("Utilio") and associated services.

---

## 1. Summary of Core Principles
* **Offline & Privacy-First Architecture:** Most tools (calculators, timers, converters, flashcards, notes, drawing pad, and local trackers) function entirely on your device without sending your data to external servers.
* **No Unnecessary Permissions:** We only request hardware and software permissions that are strictly essential for features you actively use.
* **No Data Selling:** We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Voluntarily Provide
* **Google Account Information (Optional):** If you choose to sign in with Google to enable cloud backup and synchronize your favorite tools, we receive basic profile information authorized by you:
  * Name and Email address
  * Profile picture
  * Birthday (optional, requested solely for age calculation and birthday countdowns)
* **User-Generated Content:** Notes, to-dos, habits, custom budget records, and event schedules are stored locally in your device's private storage (and optionally in your private Google Drive AppData folder if cloud sync is active).

### B. Information Collected Automatically
* **Device & Usage Data:** Basic device information (operating system version, device model, language preferences) to ensure correct rendering and layout scaling.
* **Ad Identifiers:** If you are using the ad-supported free version, Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) may process non-personalized or personalized advertising identifiers in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

---

## 3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

Utilio requests only minimal, necessary permissions:

| Permission | Category | Purpose in Utilio |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` / `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` | Location | Used exclusively on-device to retrieve localized weather forecasts, calculate sunrise/sunset times, digital compass accuracy, and accurate Islamic prayer times (Salah). Your location is never tracked continuously in the background or shared with third parties. |
| `CAMERA` | Camera | Used exclusively in the Universal Scanner tool to scan QR codes and barcodes in real-time. No photos or video recordings are stored or transmitted. |
| `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / Photo Library | Photos / Storage | Used exclusively when you choose to save custom QR codes or sketches from the Drawing Pad into your device's photo gallery, or attach an image to an event. |

> **Note:** Utilio does **NOT** request or use microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`) permissions. Audio playback is strictly one-way for ambient sounds and meditation bells.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & SDKs

We integrate with trusted service providers to deliver specialized features:

* **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob):** Serves banner and interstitial ads in the free version. You can opt out of ads completely via the in-app Ad-Free Pro upgrade.
  * [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* **Google Drive API (AppData):** Used exclusively to save and restore your pinned feature preferences to your private cloud storage sandbox. We cannot access your personal Google Drive documents.
* **Open-Meteo API:** Used for weather forecasts and air quality metrics (anonymous coordinate queries without personal identification).
* **Yahoo Finance API:** Used to retrieve live public market indices and stock quotes (anonymous queries).

---

## 5. Data Retention & Account / Data Deletion

* **Local Data:** You can clear all cached and stored app data at any time via **Settings → Clear Cache** or by clearing the app data in Android system settings.
* **Cloud Sync & Account Deletion:** If you have signed in with Google and synchronized your preferences, you can permanently delete your synchronized data and sign out directly inside the app:
  * Open **Settings → DATA & PRIVACY → Delete Account & Cloud Data**.
  * Alternatively, you can revoke Utilio's access at any time via [Google Account Security Settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
  * You may also contact us at `tanimsarwar@gmail.com` to request manual deletion of any associated records.
